q. What is the title of the book in the document?  47 Sec.
a. The title of the book is **A Bantam Spectra Book**.

output Wrong
But the first chunk likely contains multiple lines, for example:  

A Storm of Swords
George R. R. Martin
A Bantam Spectra Book
November 2000

When the LLM sees this, it sometimes misinterprets the publisher line as the title.
So the problem is now not retrieval, but context structure.

Changes made.
switched to SentenceTransformer("all-mpnet-base-v2") # 768 dimensions
Chunking: We switched from paragraph/sentence-based chunking (many small header chunks + paragraph-merge for the body) 
to a simple rule: first 500 chars = one chunk (title block),
rest = 600 chars with 150 overlap (sliding window), so there are fewer chunks and ingest is much faster.
